## Verified statements used in the article

- Publication date: **2026-10-03**.
- Product and event: Vision Group says YOTTA 2026 marked the **first public appearance of LiLic Sidecar**.
- Power: in a five-module operating configuration, Vision says **each cabinet delivers 1 MW of rated discharge power**.
- Duration and load: Vision says the same configuration provides **at least 60 seconds of backup at a 1 MW load**.
- Chemistry: Vision describes a hybrid design combining **lithium-ion capacitors (LIC)** and **lithium iron phosphate (LFP)** batteries.
- Bus architecture: Vision says LiLic Sidecar connects directly to an **800 V DC busbar (±400 V)**.
- BBU: Vision says its YOTTA portfolio included **battery backup units (BBU)** and separately describes a rack-side 72 kW, 2RU BBU with 5+1 redundancy. The article does not imply that this BBU specification is the LiLic Sidecar cabinet specification.

## Publication boundary

All numerical values, compatibility statements and system functions are attributed to Vision Group. They are not used as evidence that the figures are industry-wide, sufficient for a complete data-center design, independently validated, or achievable by Viking materials. The source does not establish that AGM/VRLA is suitable or unsuitable for a particular project, and it does not show that Viking products are used in or compatible with LiLic Sidecar.
